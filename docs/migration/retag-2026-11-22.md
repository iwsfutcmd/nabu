# Drafted tags gone over with the tagging rule (2026-11-22)

The owner asked that the drafted part-of-speech tags be gone over with the rule the lexical model
adopted: a lexeme takes one Universal Dependencies tag, the one whose dimensions it inflects for;
where nothing inflects, the tag its distribution in its own language gives; never its English
gloss's. A participle is a form of its verb and has the verb's tag. Every tag below is the owner's
to review; nothing here is signed off.

The candidates came from a count of every lexeme whose tokens choose a feature its tag's entry in
`lexicons/<lang>/language.yaml` `dimensions` does not list (es, apc, hbo, fa; chg, grc, lzh, uz and
yue showed nothing, and tok was not to be touched). Each was checked against every sentence and
story line that writes it. Ids did not change, so no alias table and no audio item changed with
them.

Contrast sets: none of these retags changes what the profiles' contrast sets reach. apc's
`verb-prefix` forms name no tag (`{Mood: Imp, Gender: Fem}`, `{Mood: Imp, Number: Plur}`, `{Aspect:
"Imp,Perf"}`), so tfaddali, leke, 3emli, tkhafi and the plurals were reached before and still are;
the bare imperative to one man is left out for every verb (the profile's REVIEW note). Its
`agreement` set reaches inflected words, and each of these lexemes already had inflected tokens.
hbo's `agreement` set reaches barukh's participles by `{VerbForm: Part}` with or without a tag, and
kol and meat stand in no feminine or plural form. es's sets name no forms.

The smaller route for khayef. The full one would make the lexeme the verb itself, `lexeme:khaf`
(خاف, khaf, "fear, be afraid"), with khayef, khayfe and tkhafi as its forms. The course never
writes khaf, so its word card would show a lemma with no clip, and the clip would have to be made
with the course's voice (OmniVoice). It would also take: the lexicon entry renamed and respelled,
its gloss and notes rewritten for the verb, the node in units/u20-celebrations.yaml that introduces
`lexeme:khayef` pointing at `lexeme:khaf`, every token's `lexeme`, `courses/apc/aliases.yaml`
(`sense:khayef` and any other right-hand side naming it), the audio item `lexeme:khayef` in
courses/apc/audio/index.yaml regenerated as `lexeme:khaf`, `pnpm bundles`. As it stands the
lexeme is a VERB spelled as its participle, which the word card shows. hbo's barukh is the same
case (a VERB spelled as its passive participle; the qal of בָּרַךְ is hardly attested apart from it).

What a retag changes for learners, to look at: a gap-fill's wrong options share the answer's tag
and form signature. Adnominal mucho is now offered beside determiners of its kind (otro, from unit
23), where before it was offered beside adverbs; ¿Cuántos ...? beside other interrogative
determiners; encantado beside adjectives; tfaddal, lek and 3mol-ma3rouf beside imperatives to one
man (eshrab, kol, wa22ef); kol and meat beside construct nouns. No story question named any of them
as a gap or a distractor, so no authored option needed replacing. `pnpm courses` gave the same
diagnostics before and after, the simulation and pacing tests pass with nothing retuned, and the
core's golden files (fa only) did not move.

## Dimensions completed

`dimensions` is read only by the Data page's Language view (`packages/core/src/lesson/lexicon-data.ts`
lists the key; nothing in the compiler, core or the UI uses its values). Each language file now
lists what its tokens choose, so the count shows nothing of this kind.

- es: VERB adds Gender and Polarity; PRON is [Gender, Number]; NUM is [Gender, Number].
- apc: PRON is [Gender, Number].
- hbo: VERB adds Voice; PRON is [Gender, Number]; DET is [Gender, Number].
- fa: AUX is [Tense, Polarity].

After these, the count lists five apc lexemes and nothing else: kell-sene and the four formulas
with a possessor's features.
